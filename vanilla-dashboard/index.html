Clinician Testing Session

Thank you for participating. This session takes about 10 minutes and focuses on how clearly the dashboard supports your clinical review and discharge-readiness interpretation.

What to Do

  • Select a patient and review the timeline, summary, and evidence panels.
  • Think aloud: share what you are looking for, what you expect, and what is unclear.
  • Complete the assigned tasks as you normally would in practice.

Notes

  • We are testing the interface, not your performance.
  • There are no right or wrong answers.
  • If something is confusing, please mention it.

Your feedback will be used to improve clinical usability and workflow fit.
